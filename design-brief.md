# You Mode — Design Brief

## What it is

You Mode (youmode.app) is a mobile-first accountability app. You tell it a goal by talking to it, it builds you a daily challenge, and you prove you showed up every day with photo evidence or simple checkmarks until you finish. It started as a hardcore "75 Hard" tracker for two guys and is being reshaped into something warm and personal enough for anyone, including design-conscious women who would screenshot a pretty app and post it.

The signature moment: a new user taps a big glowing mic and rambles about their goal for up to 10 minutes. An AI listens, asks one or two questions, then hands them a fully built challenge they can tweak and start that day.

## Who it needs to impress

Women in their 20s-40s who care about aesthetics (think Pinterest, Strong Girl Society, wellness influencers). It should feel like a beautiful personal ritual, not a military bootcamp app. Screenshot-worthy is the bar: someone should WANT to post their Day 30 screen.

## Current structure (screen by screen)

1. **Landing (web)** — one screen: brand mark, "Turn your goal into a daily challenge," three sentences, one Get Started button, Log in top right. Warm dark "sunrise" palette: near-black ember background, coral, amber, soft gold.
2. **Sign up / Log in** — username, email, phone, password. Same sunrise styling.
3. **Voice onboarding** — headline "What do you want to accomplish?", a big coral gradient mic button with pulse animation, live transcript filling a text box as you talk, then a chat interview with the AI, then an editable "Here's your challenge" review card (name, day count, format chips, checklist rows, optional stake).
4. **Personalization step** — pick a colorway (5 themes) and a voice (how the app talks to you: gentle Monk, supportive Coach, blunt Hard Core).
5. **Share code** — big gold invite code, copy button, "Start Day 1."
6. **Today (home tab)** — day counter pill ("DAY 15 / 75"), a progress ring (3/6 done today), a "3 TO GO" status banner, then a bento grid of daily item tiles: photo items show the proof photo once taken (camera placeholder before), check items are full-width rows with a big checkbox. Meal photos get captions and AI-estimated protein/calories; body-goal users see a daily macro progress bar.
7. **Standings tab** — who's winning. Compact person rows: colored dot, name, days approved, today's progress, little icons per checklist item, status chip. Versus mode shows two columns with a VS divider; community mode is a ranked leaderboard of up to 12.
8. **History tab** — grid of past days (win/fail/pending), streak feel, a tile to extend your run.
9. **Goals tab** — body goals: weigh-in trend line chart, protein/calorie targets, and a chat with an AI goal coach that proposes structured plans.
10. **Proof viewer** — tap a person's day to see their proof photos (or private icon-only tiles), referee can approve/reject.
11. **You sheet** (tap avatar) — colorway picker with paint-chip swatches, voice picker, photo privacy controls, recovery email, delete account.

## Current visual language

- **Sunrise first impression**: ember #130C08 background, coral #FF7A55, amber #FFB25C, gold #FFD9A8. Gradient CTA pills with a soft glow.
- **Five colorways** the user picks from: Midnight (near-black, red/green accents, tall condensed display type, the original hardcore look), Espresso (warm dark), Navy (dark blue with pink/blue accents), Sand (warm cream light mode), and Blush (the feminine one: cream #F8F1EA background, white cards, deep indigo text #3B3A6E, dusty rose #A34A60 accent, pastel bento tiles in peach/lavender/baby blue/mint/pink, elegant Playfair Display serif headlines, rounder corners).
- Typography elsewhere: tall condensed display numerals for big stats (day counts, scores), uppercase condensed labels.
- Cards and tiles everywhere; rounded corners (14-22px); soft borders on dark themes, colored pastel fills on Blush.

## What to explore (directions for concepts)

- Push the Blush/feminine direction further: editorial serif type, pastel bento dashboards, delicate progress rings, celebratory Day-complete moments (confetti, sunrise burst).
- A more luxurious dark mode: glassy cards, warm glow, gold accents, dawn gradient backgrounds.
- The voice moment as theater: full-screen listening state with organic waveform or breathing gradient while you talk.
- Progress as jewelry: streak calendars, medals, day counters people want to screenshot and share.
- Keep it calm and warm. No aggressive red, no drill-sergeant energy, no cluttered gamification.
