Privacy Policy

Effective July 12, 2026

You Mode is a build-your-own accountability challenge app. You create a daily checklist, log proof, and share progress with the friends and referee in your challenge. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the control you have over it. We keep it plain because the app is small and we don't sell anything.

What we collect

  • Account details — the username, cell phone number, and password you provide when you sign up. Your password is stored securely (hashed) by our authentication provider and is never visible to us. Your phone number is used only for challenge-related account matters, never marketing.
  • Challenge content you create — the proof photos you upload, checkmarks you tick, meal descriptions you write, weigh-ins you log, and goals you set.
  • Basic technical data — standard request logs (e.g. timestamps) needed to operate and secure the service. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How we use it

  • To run your challenge: show your progress, your challenge-mates' progress, and let a referee review days.
  • To provide the optional AI features: an automated spot-check that flags whether a proof photo appears to match its requirement, and macro estimates from your meal photos and descriptions.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

AI processing

When you use the AI features, the relevant proof photo and/or the meal description you wrote is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the flag or estimate, then the result is returned to the app. This data is processed to produce your result and is not used to train models. The AI's output is an estimate, not medical or nutritional advice.

Who can see your data

  • Your challenge-mates — people in the same challenge can see your day-by-day progress. What they see of your photos depends on your Proof Photos setting: "icons only" shows them your progress icons and captions but not the images; "everyone in your challenge" lets them open your photos. You can also lock individual items (like a progress photo) to stay private.
  • Your referee — if your challenge has one, the referee always sees your proof photos, because judging a day requires seeing the evidence.
  • Weigh-in numbers are visible only to you and your referee unless you choose to share them.
  • We never sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with our infrastructure providers (Supabase for the database and file storage, Netlify for hosting). Proof photos live in a private storage bucket accessed through time-limited links.

Your controls

  • Adjust who sees your photos any time in the app's settings (tap your avatar → You).
  • Edit or remove content you've logged.
  • Delete your account from within the app, which removes your profile and the challenge content tied to it. You can also request deletion by contacting us.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile and associated challenge content are removed from our systems.

Children

You Mode is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll revise the effective date above and, for material changes, note it in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@youmode.app.
